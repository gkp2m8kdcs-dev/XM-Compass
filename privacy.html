Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

The short version: MTAtlas does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal financial data. All transactions, receipts, budgets, goals, and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive your data, and we do not require an account.

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to MTAtlas, an iPhone application published under the name MTAtlas (the "App", "we", "us", or "our"). It describes how the App handles information when you install, open, and use it.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the terms set out in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not install or use the App.

2. Data We Collect

We collect nothing. MTAtlas is designed to operate without transmitting your personal data to any server operated by us or by a third party on our behalf.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your financial transactions, account names, balances, or amounts.
  • Receipt photos or other attachments you add to transactions.
  • Budget figures, goal targets, or category structures.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details, except the address you voluntarily provide when you write to our support inbox.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or persistent identifiers that link activity across apps or services.
  • Precise or coarse location data.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

To be explicit: MTAtlas does not link to your bank, brokerage, or any financial institution. It does not request, store, or transmit account credentials, OAuth tokens, or any form of authentication that would grant access to your money on the open internet. It does not perform aggregation, scoring, profiling, or marketing based on your financial activity.

4. Device Permissions

The App may request the following iOS permissions. Each permission is opt-in, requested only when you perform an action that requires it, and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.

4.1 Camera

Used solely to capture receipt photos that you choose to attach to a transaction. Photos are saved to the App's local sandbox. The App never uploads photos to a remote server.

4.2 Photo Library

Used solely to let you pick existing receipt photos from your library to attach to a transaction. The App reads only the items you select.

4.3 Notifications

Used solely to deliver local notifications, such as a reminder you set or an alert you configured for a budget threshold. All notifications are generated locally on your device.

5. Local Storage

All App data is stored locally on your iPhone inside the App's sandboxed container. The data is protected by iOS's standard app sandbox and, on supported devices, by the device passcode or biometric authentication.

If you enable iCloud Backup for your device, your device's iCloud backup may include the App's local data, because iCloud Backup is a system-level service managed by Apple, not by us. You can disable iCloud Backup for the App, or for the device as a whole, in iOS Settings. We do not control those system settings.

Uninstalling the App deletes its sandboxed container, which removes all locally stored data.

6. Exports

You may export your data at any time as a .csv or .json file through the in-app export action. Exports are produced on-device and saved to a location you choose (for example, the iOS Files app, Mail, or AirDrop). Once an export leaves your device, its handling is governed by the tools and recipients you use; we have no visibility into that flow.

7. Analytics & Crash Reporting

MTAtlas does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or behavioral tracking. Crash reports, if any, are generated and stored entirely on-device by Apple's standard iOS diagnostic facilities; you can manage those in iOS Settings → Privacy → Analytics.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal information at all, this risk does not arise in practice.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we will surface a notice inside the App. Your continued use of the App after a change indicates that you accept the updated Policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please write to us at edible197@gmail.com. We read every message.
