Before you write A two-minute checklist that solves most issues.

  1. Update the app. Open the App Store → tap your profile → scroll to MTAtlas → Update. Many issues are fixed in recent releases.
  2. Restart the app. Swipe MTAtlas up from the app switcher, then reopen it. iOS clears transient state on a fresh launch.
  3. Reboot the device. Restarting your iPhone clears low-level iOS issues that can affect any app, including MTAtlas.
  4. Check storage. Settings → General → iPhone Storage. MTAtlas stores receipts locally; very low free space can prevent photos from saving.
  5. Check permissions. Settings → MTAtlas. If Camera or Photos are disabled, receipt capture won't work; re-enable them in iOS Settings.
  6. Export your data. Before any troubleshooting that might involve reinstalling, use in-app Export to save a CSV or JSON copy.

Frequently asked questions

Does MTAtlas connect to my bank? ▾
No. MTAtlas never asks for, stores, or transmits your bank credentials, account numbers, or routing information. It is a manual-entry tracker — everything you record is typed by you, on your device.
Where is my data stored? ▾
All transactions, receipts, budgets, goals, accounts, and preferences are stored locally inside the App's iOS sandbox on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive your data.
How do I back up my data? ▾
Use the in-app Export action to save a CSV or JSON copy. If your device is configured for iCloud Backup, the App's local data may also be included in those system-level backups; you can manage that in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Backups. We do not control iCloud Backup behavior.
How do I move my data to a new iPhone? ▾
Export your data from the old device as a CSV or JSON file (AirDrop, Mail, or iCloud Drive work well). Reinstall MTAtlas on the new device, then import the file using the in-app Import action. MTAtlas does not sync across devices automatically.
Can I use MTAtlas on iPad or Mac? ▾
MTAtlas is iPhone-only. It runs on iPad only if installed via the App Store's "iPhone Apps on iPad" compatibility mode, which is not officially supported and may have visual or functional quirks.
How do I delete all my data? ▾
Open MTAtlas → Settings → Erase All Data, then confirm. This clears every transaction, receipt, budget, goal, account, and preference from the App. The action is irreversible — export first if you want a copy.
The app crashes when I attach a photo. What should I try? ▾
First, run through the "Before you write" checklist above. If the crash reproduces after an update and a device reboot, send us an email with the iOS version (Settings → General → About → Software Version), the MTAtlas version (shown in Settings inside the App), and the moment the crash happens. We investigate every reproducible report.
Is there a subscription or in-app purchase? ▾
Pricing and any optional purchases, if offered, are presented inside the App and on the App Store listing. The current Privacy Policy and Terms of Use describe the rules that apply regardless of how the App is obtained.
How do I report a security concern? ▾
Please email edible197@gmail.com with the subject line "Security" and a clear description. We acknowledge security reports promptly and treat them as a priority.
