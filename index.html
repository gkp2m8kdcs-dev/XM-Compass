Designed for everyday money

Six surfaces, one calm app. MTAtlas focuses on what you actually do with money each week — not on dashboards you'll never open again.

＋

Record in seconds

Income, expense, or transfer — pick a category, attach up to six receipt photos from the camera or library, add notes, location, and tags. Save.

◐

Envelope budgets

Set a monthly limit per category. Watch a burndown curve compare your actual pace to an ideal line, and get an alert when you cross your own threshold.

◎

Goals that move

Pick a name, a target, and a date. MTAtlas computes how much you must save per day. Milestones appear automatically; progress rings update the moment you contribute.

▦

Accounts that respect transfers

Cash, checking, savings, and credit cards with running balances that respect transfers in and out — no double counting.

⌗

Analytics that go deeper

Cash-flow trends, top merchants, category breakdowns, and a heatmap of your daily activity — all drawn by hand in SwiftUI Canvas.

⇩

Export in two taps

CSV or JSON. Wipe everything with a single confirmation. You always have an exit ramp.

Privacy by design

Your data never leaves your iPhone.

MTAtlas is offline-first by design. No bank linking, no account, no cloud sync. Every transaction, receipt photo, budget, and goal lives on your device — and only on your device.

  • ✓
    No bank connections.MTAtlas never asks for, stores, or transmits your bank credentials.
  • ✓
    No account required.Open the app and start tracking. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.
  • ✓
    No server sync.All records, attachments, and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone.
  • ✓
    Export & erase anytime.Take your data as CSV or JSON. Wipe everything with a single confirmation.
